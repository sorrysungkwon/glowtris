About Glowtris

Glowtris — Free Neon Block-Stacking Puzzle Game

Glowtris is a free, neon block-stacking puzzle game that runs in any browser with no download required. Stack falling neon blocks, clear lines, and compete on a global leaderboard — all for free, with no ads.

Game Modes

  • Daily Challenge — Every player gets the same block sequence each day. Compete on today's leaderboard and see where you rank globally.
  • Marathon — Classic block-stacking endless mode. How long can you survive as the blocks fall faster?
  • Sprint — Clear 40 lines as fast as possible. Chase your personal best and compare with the all-time leaderboard.
  • Blitz — Score as many points as you can in 2 minutes.

Features

  • Free to play — no account, no download, no ads
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers
  • Installs as a PWA (Progressive Web App) on your home screen
  • Global online leaderboard updated in real time
  • Daily challenge — same board for every player worldwide
  • Push notifications for daily challenge reminders
  • Neon visual style with smooth 60fps gameplay
If you enjoy block-stacking games, block puzzle games, or competitive browser games, Glowtris offers a fresh neon take with daily challenges and sprint mode. Play now at glowtris.com — no sign-up needed. Read the Glowtris dev blog for behind-the-scenes updates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Glowtris?

Glowtris is a free neon block-stacking puzzle game that runs in any browser with no download or account required. Stack falling neon blocks, clear lines, and compete on a global leaderboard — all for free, with no ads.

What game modes does Glowtris have?

Glowtris has four modes: Daily Challenge (every player gets the same block sequence each day), Marathon (endless classic block-stacking), Sprint (clear 40 lines as fast as possible), and Blitz (score as many points as you can in 2 minutes).

Is Glowtris free to play?

Yes. Glowtris is completely free to play with no ads, no account, and no download required. It runs in any modern browser on desktop or mobile.

Can I play Glowtris on mobile?

Yes. Glowtris works on mobile browsers and can be installed as a PWA (Progressive Web App) directly on your iOS or Android home screen for an app-like experience.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Every day at midnight UTC, Glowtris generates a new Daily Challenge using a fixed random seed. Every player worldwide gets the exact same sequence of blocks, making it a fair global competition. Your score is recorded on today's leaderboard.
